Bible Hover is a free and 100% open-source Chrome extension that transforms how you read scripture on the web. Whenever you visit any webpage, article, sermon, or study guide, Bible Hover automatically detects Bible references and displays instant, elegant hover preview cards with zero interruption to your reading.

No ads. No tracking. No subscriptions. 100% Free & Open Source under the GNU General Public License (GPLv3).

• Bundled Berean Standard Bible (BSB): The complete Bible is pre-bundled directly within the extension. Hover previews appear in 0 milliseconds with zero network requests and zero latency.
• Complete Offline Capability: Works entirely offline on flights, commutes, or areas with poor internet connection.
• Offline Bibles Manager: Easily download additional full translations (such as the King James Version) into local browser storage with 1-click management and instant removal.

Choose from 4 flexible commentary display modes:
1. Direct 1-Click Link: Instant button to open chapter/verse commentary on BibleHub or TGC.
2. In-Card Interactive Tabs: Scrollable commentary tabs right inside the hover card.
3. Multi-Action Resource Pills: Quick-access buttons for Commentaries, TGC, TheSinging.Bible (audio singing scripture), and YouTube sermons.
4. Side Drawer Reader: Slide out an unobtrusive reading drawer to examine extensive study notes side-by-side with your article.

🎨 CUSTOM TYPOGRAPHY & ACCESSIBILITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Bible Hover features selective typography: your font choice is applied exclusively to the sacred scripture verse text, keeping buttons, headers, and UI chrome crisp and clean in modern system sans.

• Tailored Font Stacks:
  - Modern Sans (Default): Clean, neutral contemporary reading.
  - Classic Bible Serif: Traditional book typography featuring Georgia and Palatino.
  - Elegant Garamond: Distinguished, warm editorial typography.
  - Humanist Sans: Rounded, gentle letterforms (Calibri / Candara) that reduce eye fatigue.
  - Dyslexia-Friendly: Research-backed accessibility stack (OpenDyslexic, Comic Sans, Verdana) with +0.04em letter-spacing, +0.08em word-spacing, and 1.82 line-height to reduce visual crowding and character confusion.
  - Study Monospace: Fixed-width code typography for analytical cross-referencing.
• 4 Comfortable Color Themes: Auto (System match), Crisp Light, OLED Dark, and Warm Sepia (Book Style).
• Pure Scripture Mode (Minimalist): Strip away buttons, links, and chrome for a clean, distraction-free reading experience.
• Text Size Control: Compact (13px), Default (14.5px), and Spacious (16px).

💡 HOW TO USE BIBLE HOVER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. Install the extension.
2. Browse any webpage, blog post, theological essay, or commentary that mentions scripture references (e.g., Romans 6:7, John 3:16, or Genesis 1:1-3).
3. Simply hover your cursor over the highlighted reference to view the verse instantly.
4. Click translation pills to compare translations, or click the commentary button to delve deeper into study notes!
