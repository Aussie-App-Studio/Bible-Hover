Standard & Chained References

The core promise of the Gospel is declared in John 3:16-18, which tells us that God so loved the world that He gave His one and only Son. We also see assurance in Romans 8:28, 31–39; and the Apostle Paul continues this exhortation in 12:1-2.

Poetry & Single-Chapter Books

Consider the comfort found in Psalm 23, and the profound discourse on love in 1 Cor 13:4-8a. Furthermore, small epistles such as Jude 3-4 and Philemon 6 are fully supported even without specifying a chapter number.
