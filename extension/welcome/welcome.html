What Bible Hover Does For You

Bible Hover transforms any webpage into a distraction-free study Bible. Whenever you browse articles, sermons, study notes, or blogs, it automatically detects Scripture references (e.g. Romans 6:1-7, John 3:16 NIV, Psalm 23) and displays instant 0ms verse popups with no page loading. You can switch translations on the fly (BSB, KJV, ESV, NIV, NLT, NASB, CSB, NKJV, and international bibles), copy verses with one click, or jump straight into verse-by-verse commentaries (Matthew Henry, David Guzik, John Calvin, Constable, TGC)—all running locally in your browser with complete privacy.

Why the Berean Standard Bible (BSB) is the Default

Instant 0ms scripture popups with zero copyright restrictions—plus how to easily change your default and download any translation for offline 0ms speed.

🏛️
Copyright Freedom

Commercial Copyright vs Public Domain

Beloved modern translations like the ESV (Crossway), NIV (Biblica), and NASB (Lockman) are subject to strict commercial copyrights. Bundling their full text pre-packaged inside a free extension binary without licensing agreements is legally restricted.

The Berean Standard Bible (BSB) is graciously dedicated to the public domain (Berean.Bible), allowing Bible Hover to bundle all 66 books directly into the extension 100% legally, forever free, and royalty-free.
⚡
Zero Server Latency

Pre-Bundled for Instant 0ms Popups

Because the complete BSB is stored directly on your computer inside the extension, hovering over any scripture reference displays verses in 0 milliseconds.

There is zero waiting for external web servers or APIs, no loading spinners or bandwidth delay, and complete reading privacy with no network tracking.

🛡️ Thoughtful Defaults • Full Control

Where Bible Hover Pauses by Default (And Why)

To keep your browsing clean and distraction-free, Bible Hover automatically starts turned Off on dedicated Bible study platforms and document editors—while letting you turn it back On anytime with 1 click.

📖 Dedicated Bible Platforms

Sites like Bible.com, BibleGateway, BibleHub, OpenBible, Blue Letter Bible, and ESV.org already display full Scripture passages or have their own built-in cross-reference popups. Pausing by default prevents redundant double-popups over chapters you are already reading.

📝 Google Docs & Spreadsheets

When you are actively writing sermons, study notes, or documents in Google Docs (docs.google.com) or Google Sheets, Bible Hover stays out of the way by default so it never interrupts your typing or cursor.
